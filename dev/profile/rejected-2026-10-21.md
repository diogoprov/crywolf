# Speed experiments not adopted (21/10/2026, crywolf 1.5.0)

All on an M5 Pro, CmdStan with `stan_threads`, 4 chains, 1000 + 1000
iterations, `adapt_delta = 0.95`. Curiango: custom classifier, threshold
0.40, `occ = ~ temp + rh + moon`, `det = ~ bio + aci`, `fp = ~ 1`, recorder
effect (7 recorders), 4 threads per chain. Simulations: `simulate_fp()`
defaults with the recorder settings shown, 1 thread. ESS/s is the smallest
bulk ESS among `beta`, `alpha`, `fp_gap`, `gamma0`, `sigma_u` divided by the
wall time of the slowest chain.

Baseline curiango: 393 s, warm-up about 150 s and sampling about 230 s per
chain, 105 leapfrogs per iteration (treedepth 6.3), posterior correlation of
`fp_gap` and `det_(Intercept)` 0.99; no other pair above 0.5. Repeating the
same configuration gave ESS/s of 2.40, 1.59, and 2.32: differences below
about 30% are noise.

## Rejected

- **Pathfinder or MAP initialization.** Warm-up is only 40% of the time on
  the curiango, so a shorter warm-up could save at most a fraction of that,
  at the risk of worse adaptation. Not pursued.
- **QR decomposition of the design matrices.** No posterior correlation
  among the covariate effects exceeded 0.5, so there is nothing for QR to
  remove.
- **Dense mass matrix (`metric = "dense_e"`, can be passed through `...`).**
  Curiango: 161 s instead of 393 s, 42 leapfrogs, but 6 divergent
  transitions and half the ESS of `sd_recorder` (513 vs 1163); ESS/s 3.18 vs
  2.40. Not a safe default with a hierarchical recorder effect.
- **Sampling the false-positive intercept with the gap** (alpha[1] =
  gamma0 + gap, unit Jacobian; log density equal to 1e-13, draws identical
  with it off). With a recorder effect the detection intercept is the
  uncertain one (sd 0.44 vs 0.07 on the curiango; same pattern in
  simulations with 5 to 30 recorders), but the leapfrogs did not fall
  (curiango 108 -> 126) and ESS/s changed within the noise.
- **Centred recorder effect** (data-rich recorders), alone or with the
  above: curiango ESS/s 2.32 / 2.46 / 2.60 / 1.80 for the four
  combinations; simulations with 5, 10, and 30 recorders mixed, no
  combination best everywhere.

Earlier rejections: larger `grainsize` (1.3.0), vector intercept without the
scalar branch (1.3.0), another language (1.1.0).

## Threads and reproducibility

One chain on the curiango, 150 iterations from a posterior draw with the
adapted step size and metric of the baseline fit (no adaptation):

| reduce_sum | 1 thread | 2 | 4 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dynamic, grainsize 1 (current) | 55.1 s | 28.8 s | 16.1 s | 10.3 s |
| static, grainsize 28 | 44.1 s | 21.8 s | 12.8 s | 7.8 s |

`reduce_sum_static()` was tried in the hope that a fixed partition would
make the draws independent of the number of threads. It does not: with
grainsize 28, 55, 110, or 220 the draws with 2, 4, or 8 threads differ from
those with 1, and with the dynamic version two runs with the same seed and 4
(or 8) threads differ from each other. Times of the static runs beyond one
thread are not comparable, since the trajectories diverge. Kept: the
dynamic version, 1 thread by default (exact reproduction by seed), and
`threads_per_chain = "auto"` as an option.

## Fixed cost per fit (calibration designs)

Median of three fits, `simulate_fp()` designs, chains in parallel:

| design | chains | wall | sampler | overhead | min bulk ESS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 sites, 6 occasions | 2 | 6.6 s | 6.2 s | 0.4 s | 1919 |
| 300 sites, 20 occasions | 2 | 21.2 s | 20.8 s | 0.5 s | 2175 |
| 1000 sites, 30 occasions | 2 | 66.1 s | 65.4 s | 0.7 s | 1994 |
| 100 sites, 6 occasions | 4 | 7.7 s | 7.1 s | 0.6 s | 4276 |
| 300 sites, 20 occasions | 4 | 24.4 s | 23.4 s | 1.0 s | 4520 |
| 1000 sites, 30 occasions | 4 | 71.3 s | 70.2 s | 1.1 s | 4150 |

The CmdStan process and reading its output cost under 1.2 s per fit, so
they are not worth optimizing. In calibrations, where each worker runs the
chains of a fit one after another, the number of chains sets the cost;
documented in `fp_calibrate()` and the calibration vignette.

## Mixing with `random_fp = TRUE`

20 datasets of the unstructured regime (`simulate_fp(nsite = 300, nocc = 20,
sigma_p10 = 1, frac_verified = 0.3)`), `occ = ~ x`, `fp = ~ 1`, 4 chains in
sequence, 6 fits in parallel (some timings overlap with other work):

| setting | fits with R-hat > 1.01 | median time | median min bulk ESS | median ESS/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| default (1000 warm-up, adapt_delta 0.95) | 3 of 20 | 227 s | 688 | 3.2 |
| iter_warmup = 2000 | 2 of 20 | 297 s | 684 | 2.4 |
| adapt_delta = 0.99 | 3 of 20 | 355 s | 634 | 2.1 |

No divergent transitions in any fit. A longer warm-up or a higher
`adapt_delta` costs more than it gains, so the defaults stay; the
occasional R-hat above 1.01 is flagged by `fp_occu()`, and more
`iter_sampling` is the remedy. Marginalizing the site effects by quadrature
was not pursued.
